🔥 OUR ORIGIN STORY

From a Street Food Truck to Burger Royalty

BurgerHub was born in 2018 in a modest food truck parked by the Mumbai seaside. We were tired of dry, factory-frozen patties stuffed with preservatives. We believed a burger should be a culinary masterpiece: searing heat, caramelized crust, decadent cheese, and pillows of buttery brioche.

Today, our flat-top grills sear hundreds of fresh burgers every day, yet our commitment has never changed: every patty is freshly ground, every sauce is simmered from scratch, and every customer leaves smiling.
